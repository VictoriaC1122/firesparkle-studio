資料儲存 / Local storage
任務、用藥紀錄、每日紀錄、照護對象、預約、筆記與草稿保存在裝置上。App 不需要帳號，沒有廣告、分析或追蹤 SDK，也沒有由工作室提供的雲端同步。
Tasks, medication logs, daily records, care profiles, appointments, notes and drafts stay on your device. No account is required. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK and no studio-operated cloud sync.
匯出、還原與刪除 / Export, restore and deletion
你可匯出 JSON 或 CSV，並以 JSON 還原資料。匯出檔案可能包含健康及他人資料，請自行保管。App 資料排除系統備份；刪除 App 或遺失裝置可能造成資料遺失。刪除 App 內資料不會刪除已分享的副本、支援郵件或 Apple 交易紀錄。
You can export JSON or CSV and restore a JSON backup. Exports may contain health information or other people’s details; keep them private. App data is excluded from system backups. Deleting the app or losing the device may lose your records. In-app deletion does not remove shared copies, support emails or Apple transaction records.
Apple 服務與通知 / Apple services and notifications
購買與訂閱由 Apple StoreKit 處理，App 讀取交易狀態以判定 Pro 權限，不會取得付款卡號。裝置驗證使用 Apple 系統服務，工作室不會取得生物辨識資料或裝置密碼。提醒由本機通知處理；通知不顯示藥名、劑量或健康紀錄。
Apple StoreKit handles purchases and subscriptions. The app reads transaction status for Pro access and does not receive payment-card numbers. Device authentication uses Apple services; the studio receives no biometric data or device passcode. Reminders are local notifications and do not display medication names, doses or health records.
外部連結與支援 / External links and support
App 內官方 PDF 隨程式提供。開啟外部網站時，該網站依自己的政策處理連線資料。寄信聯絡工作室時，我們會收到你的信箱、訊息及附件，僅用於回覆、排除問題或處理隱私請求，保留至處理及必要後續聯繫完成。信箱由 Gmail 提供，網站由 GitHub Pages 提供，其服務政策另行適用。
Official PDFs are bundled with the app. External websites handle connection data under their own policies. Support emails provide your email address, message and attachments; we use them to respond, troubleshoot or handle privacy requests and retain them through necessary follow-up. Gmail hosts support email and GitHub Pages hosts this site; their policies apply separately.
照護資料與聯絡 / Care information and contact
記錄他人資訊前，請取得適當同意並只填寫必要內容。本 App 不提供兒童獨立帳號或兒童診斷問卷。資料權利請求、內容更正與支援請聯絡 firesparkle.studio@gmail.com。政策變更會在本頁標示日期；資料處理方式如有變動，會更新說明。
Obtain appropriate consent before recording another person’s information and include only necessary details. The app provides no separate child accounts or child diagnostic questionnaires. Contact firesparkle.studio@gmail.com for data requests, corrections or support. Policy changes are dated on this page; changes to data handling will be disclosed.
