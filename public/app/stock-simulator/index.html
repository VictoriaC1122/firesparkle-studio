App 介紹

關於這款 App

完整介紹與操作說明提供繁體中文與英文版本。

「股神養成」是給股市新手的模擬交易工作台。用虛擬資金練習下單、寫下每一筆的理由，再回頭檢視持倉與交易結果；基本的股票模擬與教學完全免費，不需要註冊或登入。

台股部分使用證交所與櫃買中心的開放盤後資料，可查看大盤、漲跌家數、成交排行、估值資料與除權息日程，搜尋股票與 ETF 建立自選清單，並依收盤價、自訂價格成交或建立指定價模擬委託。美股與 ETF 收盤價取自 Nasdaq.com 公開網頁，可在設定關閉自動更新；台幣與美元帳戶分開計算。

可選購的 Pro 訂閱提供獨立全螢幕期貨與選擇權工作台，包含行情下單、持倉、學習與工具。可查看期交所每日 TX、MTX、TMF、TE、TF 期貨盤後行情，以及 TXO 月份與週別選擇權鏈，再選合約帶入模擬下單。海外微型美股指數、黃金、原油期貨及標準美股／ETF 選擇權仍使用手動價格。所有下單只用虛擬資金，盤後資料不代表現在可成交價格。

Stock Mastery is a paper-trading workbench for people new to the stock market. Practise placing orders with virtual funds, write down the reason behind each trade, then go back and review your holdings and results. Basic stock simulation and the tutorials are free, with no registration or sign-in.

The Taiwan side uses open end-of-day data from the Taiwan Stock Exchange and the TPEx: market overview, advances and declines, turnover rankings, valuation data and ex-dividend schedules. Search stocks and ETFs to build a watchlist, then fill orders at the closing price or a custom price, or create a limit-price simulated order. US stock and ETF closing prices come from public Nasdaq.com pages, with automatic updates that can be disabled in Settings. The TWD and USD accounts are kept separate.

An optional Pro subscription opens a separate full-screen futures and options workspace with Markets, Positions, Learn and Tools. View daily TAIFEX end-of-day quotes for TX, MTX, TMF, TE and TF futures, plus monthly and weekly TXO option chains, then select a contract to prefill a paper order. Overseas micro US index, gold and crude oil futures and standard US stock/ETF options still use manual prices. All orders use virtual funds; end-of-day quotes are not current executable prices.

主要功能

  • 台股盤後練習證交所與櫃買中心開放資料，大盤、排行、估值與除權息一次查看；收盤價、自訂價格或指定價模擬委託。
  • 美股收盤價模擬使用 Nasdaq.com 公開網頁的美股與 ETF 收盤價，持倉依最新已取得資料估值；可在設定關閉美股自動更新。
  • 下單理由與紀錄每筆模擬交易都能寫下理由，之後對照結果回顧；紀錄保存在裝置上，不需帳號。
  • Pro 盤後期貨與選擇權鏈查看 TX、MTX、TMF、TE、TF 期貨及 TXO 月份／週別選擇權鏈，區分官方資料日期與最近成功更新時間；選合約帶入模擬下單前，確認權利金、模擬保證金、費用與可用資金。
  • 教學與查詢股票新手說明、完整操作指南、111 個常用名詞與防詐查證；Pro 另提供五章期貨與選擇權入門課，包含範例、練習題與進度保存。
  • 12 種語言與小工具介面語言可選擇或跟隨系統；新期權教學提供繁中與英文，其他語言可能顯示英文。桌面小工具提供台股盤後摘要與每日新知。
  • Taiwan after-hours practiceOpen TWSE and TPEx data with market overview, rankings, valuations and ex-dividend dates; fill at the close, a custom price or a limit-price simulated order.
  • US closing-price practiceUse US stock and ETF closing prices from public Nasdaq.com pages, with holdings valued at the latest available data. Automatic US updates can be disabled in Settings.
  • Reasons and recordsWrite a reason for every simulated trade and compare it with the outcome later; records stay on the device with no account.
  • Pro end-of-day futures and option chainsBrowse TX, MTX, TMF, TE and TF futures and monthly/weekly TXO option chains, with official data dates separate from the last successful download. Select a contract to prefill a paper order, then check premium, practice margin, fees and available funds.
  • Lessons and referenceStock starter guidance, a full manual, 111 common terms and scam-verification resources. Pro adds five beginner futures and options lessons with examples, questions and saved progress.
  • Twelve languages and widgetsChoose an interface language or follow the system. New futures and options lessons use Traditional Chinese or English, with possible English fallback in other languages. Home-screen widgets provide a Taiwan end-of-day summary and a daily lesson.

App 使用指南

使用方式

  1. 01↗

    先選擇台股或美股練習，搜尋台股或從美股內建清單選取標的，確認虛擬資金。

    第一次使用可以跟著「新手快速開始」的三個步驟，了解資金、價格與下單。台股與美股是各自獨立的練習帳戶，預設資金分別是 NT$1,000,000 與 US$100,000；台股可以搜尋股票與 ETF 加入自選，美股則從內建清單選擇標的。

    On first use, the three-step quick start explains virtual funds, prices and placing orders. Taiwan and US trading are separate practice accounts with NT$1,000,000 and US$100,000 to start; search Taiwan stocks and ETFs to add to your watchlist, and pick US symbols from the built-in list.

  2. 02↗

    選擇最新收盤價或自訂價格模擬台股與美股下單，並記錄交易理由。

    在行情、自選或個股頁查看成交價、漲跌與幅度，再從個股頁底部的「買進／賣出」進入下單。台股可依顯示的收盤價或自訂價格成交，也可以建立指定價模擬委託；美股可用最新收盤價或自訂價格下單。送出前查看底部的預估金額與可買張數，並寫下交易理由，方便日後回顧。

    Review the last price, change and percentage change in Quotes, Watchlist or a stock page, then use the Buy or Sell control pinned at the bottom of the stock page. Taiwan orders can fill at the displayed close or a custom price, or sit as a limit-price simulated order; US orders use the latest close or a custom price. Before submitting, check the estimate and how many lots you can buy, and write the reason for the trade so you can review it later.

  3. 03↗

    完成模擬後查看持倉與交易紀錄，對照當時留下的理由，回顧這筆練習的結果。

    到「我的持倉」查看成本、估值、可用資金與已實現、未實現損益，再對照交易日誌與當時的理由。使用練習任務、每週回顧及可設定的回顧提醒；到期與盤後資金條件提醒也不是即時警報。想從頭練習時，可以重設模擬帳戶。

    Open your holdings to see cost, valuation, available funds and realised and unrealised profit or loss, then review your trading journal and reasons. Use practice tasks, weekly reviews and configurable review reminders. Expiry and after-hours cash-condition reminders are not real-time alerts. Reset the practice account whenever you want to start over.

更多用法

指定價模擬委託
台股指定價單會在 App 取得後續交易日資料時，依日線高低價檢查是否成交，不重播逐筆交易。
市場資訊
查看大盤、漲跌家數、成交排行、估值資料與除權息日程，練習時把它們當作判斷依據。
Pro 全螢幕工作台
在行情下單、持倉、學習與工具之間切換，配色跟隨 App 深淺色設定。期貨與選擇權支援多空、加倉、部分及全部平倉；成交價、練習到期日、保證金與費用由使用者設定，到期仍需手動平倉。自訂情境時間暫停匯入現實行情，不會回放歷史或預測價格。
教學區
常用查詢（ETF、本益比、交割、殖利率、融資）與依主題瀏覽（第一次接觸、開戶與交易、商品全覽、公司與財報、風險管理、防詐與查證），收錄 111 個常用名詞；教學可隨時跳過，不影響模擬功能。
練習工具箱與備份
使用交易日誌、備份與還原、歷史封存及 CSV 匯出整理練習。設定可調整背景更新、語言與回顧提醒。
Pro 權益與既有紀錄
有效訂閱用於開立新的衍生品部位；權益失效後，既有紀錄仍可查看、更新參考價、平倉與匯出。基本股票模擬與教學維持免費。
Pro 風險與情境工具
使用持倉壓力測試、選擇權到期損益圖、相容既有部位的多腿策略快照、情境比較與期貨口數預算，檢視模擬假設的影響。策略快照不建立委託，也不抵銷保證金。
五章期權入門課（Pro）
依序學習商品與乘數、讀懂報價、保證金、模擬下單，以及平倉與交易紀錄。每章提供範例與練習題，並保存學習進度。
保證金與追繳試算（Pro）
了解原始、維持與結算保證金，使用明確標示為假設的追繳金額試算，並開啟官方資料連結查核。App 不執行真實追繳或強制平倉。
Limit-price simulated orders
A Taiwan limit order is checked against the daily high and low when the app receives the next trading day's data; trades are not replayed tick by tick.
Market information
Use the market overview, advances and declines, turnover rankings, valuation data and ex-dividend schedule as the basis for practice decisions.
Pro full-screen workspace
Switch between Markets, Positions, Learn and Tools in the app’s light or dark appearance. Futures and options support long and short positions, additions, and partial or full closes. You set fills, practice expiry, margin and fees; expired positions still require manual closing. Custom practice time pauses current-data imports and does not replay history or predict prices.
Learning area
Quick look-ups (ETF, P/E ratio, settlement, dividend yield, margin) and topics from first steps, accounts and trading, products, companies and financial statements, risk management to scams and verification, with 111 common terms; the tutorials can be skipped at any time without affecting the simulation.
Practice toolbox and backups
Organise practice with a trading journal, backup and restore, historical archives and CSV export. Settings controls background updates, language and review reminders.
Pro access and existing records
A valid subscription is required to open new derivatives positions. After access ends, existing records, reference-price updates, closing and export remain available; basic stock simulation and guides stay free.
Pro risk and scenario tools
Explore position stress tests, option expiry payoffs, multi-leg strategy snapshots of compatible existing positions, scenario comparisons and futures quantity budgeting. Strategy snapshots neither submit orders nor offset margin.
Five beginner lessons (Pro)
Work through contracts and multipliers, reading quotes, margin, paper orders, and closing and trade records. Lessons include examples, practice questions and saved progress.
Margin and margin-call calculator (Pro)
Learn about initial, maintenance and clearing margin, use clearly hypothetical margin-call calculations and follow official reference links. The app does not issue actual margin calls or force liquidation.

小提醒

  • 每筆模擬都寫理由，幾週後回看，比損益數字更能看出自己的習慣。
  • 行情為盤後資料，可能延遲或缺漏，請核對官方資料日期及最近成功更新時間。App 在前景檢查新資料，背景執行由 iOS 安排，不保證固定時間；下載失敗保留既有資料與日期。
  • 保證金不是最大損失，期貨與賣方選擇權的虧損可能超過保證金，練習時也請記得這點。
  • 模擬只用虛擬資金，不連接銀行或券商；真實交易請以交易所與券商的正式資訊為準。
  • 不提供即時或夜盤報價、交易所結算、履約、指派或實物交割；模擬及教學不構成個人化投資建議或獲利保證。
  • Write a reason for every simulated trade; reading them back after a few weeks reveals your habits better than the profit and loss ever will.
  • Quotes are end-of-day and may be delayed or incomplete. Check the official data date and last successful update. The app checks in the foreground; iOS controls background execution with no fixed-time guarantee. Failed downloads preserve prior data and dates.
  • Margin is not your maximum loss: futures and short options can lose more than the margin posted, in practice as in reality.
  • The simulation uses virtual funds only and connects to no bank or broker; for real trading, rely on the exchange's and your broker's official information.
  • There are no real-time or night-session quotes, exchange settlement, exercise, assignment or physical delivery. Simulations and lessons are not personal investment advice or return guarantees.
