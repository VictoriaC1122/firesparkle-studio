FinAtlas 隱私政策

文件版本：2026-11-05

FinAtlas 由花火工作室 FireSparkle.Studio 提供。本政策說明 FinAtlas App 如何處理資料，以及您主動聯絡我們時的資料處理方式。

學習資料與偏好

FinAtlas 不要求建立帳號。課程完成狀態、測驗作答紀錄、筆記、收藏、學習偏好、語言與外觀設定，以及虛構企業授信模擬紀錄，儲存在您的裝置上，用於顯示學習進度、安排課程與保留練習結果。模擬器保留最近 30 次結果；有效 Pro 訂閱期間可重新檢視。

目前 App 沒有自建後端、開發者提供的跨裝置同步功能、廣告 SDK 或第三方分析 SDK，也不會自行將上述學習資料傳送給工作室。不會把筆記或練習內容送交生成式 AI 服務。請勿在筆記或模擬欄位輸入銀行密碼、付款卡號、客戶資料或其他不必要的敏感資訊。

購買與訂閱

訂閱由 Apple 的 App Store 處理。FinAtlas 透過 StoreKit 讀取並驗證交易及訂閱狀態，以決定是否開放 Pro 功能。工作室不會透過 App 接收您的付款卡號或 Apple 帳號密碼，也沒有自行接收購買憑證的伺服器。

Apple 會依其服務條款及隱私政策處理購買、付款與相關紀錄。刪除學習資料或 App 不會取消訂閱，也不會刪除 Apple 持有的購買紀錄。訂閱管理與取消請至 Apple 帳號的訂閱設定辦理。

裝置備份

系統備份可能包含 App 的本機資料，是否備份及如何還原取決於您的 Apple 裝置、iCloud 或電腦備份設定。這與工作室提供的雲端同步不同。工作室無法存取您的裝置備份；從舊備份還原時，先前保存的資料可能再次出現。

支援信件

當您寄信至 firesparkle.studio@gmail.com，我們會收到寄件地址、您提供的姓名、信件內容及附件。您可以選擇提供裝置型號、系統版本或問題畫面，協助我們查明問題。寄信並非使用 App 的必要條件。

我們使用這些資料回覆詢問、處理問題與必要的後續聯繫，不用於廣告投放或建立行銷名單。請先遮蔽截圖中的帳號、交易資料及其他無關個人資訊。我們不會要求您提供密碼或付款卡號。

信件透過 Gmail 收發與保存，服務提供者會依其條款處理信件，處理地點可能在您所在國家或地區以外。我們只在處理問題、必要的後續追蹤及依法保存所需期間保留支援資料；目的結束後，刪除不再需要的個人資料。您也可來信要求查詢、更正或刪除您提供的支援資料；如有依法必須保留的部分，我們會說明原因。

外部網站

您點選資料來源、支援或條款連結時，會開啟第三方或工作室網站。網站及其服務提供者可能處理 IP 位址、瀏覽器資訊或伺服器紀錄，適用該網站的隱私政策。FinAtlas 不透過 App 追蹤您在外部網站的瀏覽活動。工作室官網的資料處理另見 https://firesparkle.com/website-privacy/ 。

保留與刪除

您可在「個人」頁選擇「刪除學習資料」，刪除本機筆記、收藏、課程進度、測驗及模擬紀錄。這個操作會保留語言、外觀與學習偏好；您可在個人頁調整這些設定。若要移除 App 及其本機資料，請使用 iOS 的「刪除 App」，而非保留文件資料的「卸載 App」。備份中的資料須依備份服務的設定另行管理。

我們沒有您的本機學習資料副本，因此無法代您讀取、刪除或還原這些紀錄。寄給工作室的支援信件不會隨 App 內的刪除操作移除，請另以 Email 聯絡我們。

資料分享與政策更新

工作室不出售上述個人資料，也不將其提供給資料仲介。除支援所需的服務提供者外，僅在法律要求或處理相關法律程序所必要的範圍內揭露資料。

本政策依目前功能制定。若資料處理方式改變，我們會更新政策並標示版本日期；依法需要通知或取得同意時，將在變更適用前辦理。

FinAtlas Privacy Policy

Document version: November 5, 2026

FinAtlas is provided by 花火工作室 FireSparkle.Studio. This policy describes how the FinAtlas app handles data and how we handle information you send us for support.

Learning records and preferences

FinAtlas does not require an account. Lesson completion, quiz attempts, notes, bookmarks, learning preferences, language and appearance settings, and fictional corporate credit simulation records are stored on your device. They are used to display progress, arrange lessons and retain practice results. The simulator keeps the latest 30 results, which can be reviewed while a Pro subscription is active.

The current app has no custom backend, developer-operated cross-device sync, advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. It does not itself transmit these learning records to the studio or send notes or practice content to generative AI services. Do not enter bank passwords, payment card numbers, client records or other unnecessary sensitive information in notes or simulation fields.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes subscriptions through the App Store. FinAtlas uses StoreKit to read and verify transactions and subscription status to determine access to Pro features. The studio does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password through the app and does not operate a server that receives purchase receipts.

Apple handles purchases, payments and related records under its own terms and privacy policy. Deleting learning data or the app does not cancel a subscription or delete Apple's purchase records. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Device backups

System backups may include local app data. Backup and restoration depend on your Apple device, iCloud or computer backup settings. This is separate from studio-operated cloud sync. The studio cannot access your device backups. Restoring an older backup may restore previously saved data.

Support email

When you email firesparkle.studio@gmail.com, we receive your sender address, any name you provide, your message and attachments. You may choose to include your device model, system version or screenshots to help us investigate. Contacting support is optional and is not required to use the app.

We use this information to answer inquiries, address problems and provide necessary follow-up. We do not use it for advertising or marketing lists. Please remove account details, transaction information and unrelated personal information from screenshots. We do not ask for passwords or payment card numbers.

We use Gmail to send, receive and store support messages. The provider processes messages under its terms, potentially outside your country or region. We retain support information for the time needed to resolve an issue, provide necessary follow-up and meet legal retention requirements, then delete personal information no longer needed for those purposes. You may email us to request access to, correction of or deletion of support information you supplied. If a legal requirement prevents deletion of some information, we will explain why.

External websites

Source, support and terms links open third-party or studio websites. Those sites and their providers may process IP addresses, browser information or server logs under their own privacy policies. FinAtlas does not track your browsing on external websites through the app. The studio website's policy is available at https://firesparkle.com/website-privacy/ .

Retention and deletion

In Profile, select Delete learning data to remove local notes, bookmarks, lesson progress, quiz records and simulation records. This keeps your language, appearance and learning preferences, which you can adjust in Profile. To remove the app and its local data, use Delete App in iOS, rather than Offload App, which keeps documents and data. Manage backup copies separately through your backup service settings.

We do not hold a copy of your local learning records and cannot access, delete or restore them for you. Deleting data in the app does not delete support messages you previously sent. Contact us by email for requests concerning those messages.

Sharing and changes

The studio does not sell this personal information or provide it to data brokers. Apart from providers needed to handle support, information is disclosed only where required by law or necessary for related legal proceedings.

This policy reflects the current app's features. If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its version date. Where notice or consent is legally required, we will provide it or obtain it before the change applies.
