Common questions

常見問題Frequently asked questions

如何管理訂閱或申請退款？How do I manage a subscription or ask for a refund?

若 App 提供 Apple App 內購買或訂閱，可在 iPhone「設定 → 你的姓名 → 訂閱項目」管理。退款請透過 Apple 回報問題提出，是否符合退款條件由 Apple 審核。刪除 App 不會自動取消訂閱。

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. Manage them in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are requested through Apple’s Report a Problem and reviewed by Apple. Deleting an app does not cancel its subscription.

在哪裡查看 App 的隱私政策？Where can I read an app’s privacy policy?

前往隱私中心，選擇你使用的 App。不同 App 的資料、權限及刪除方式可能不同。

Visit the privacy centre and choose your app. Data, permissions and deletion differ from app to app.

可以協助找回 App 裡的資料嗎？Can you help me recover data in an app?

請先來信說明 App 名稱與狀況。各 App 的儲存與備份功能不同，部分資料只保存在你的裝置上，開發者可能無法存取或還原；刪除或重裝前，請先確認該 App 的備份方式。

Email us with the app name and what happened. Storage and backup differ by app, and some data lives only on your device, where we cannot access or restore it. Check the app’s backup options before deleting or reinstalling.
