A little about the studio

靈感很小。
日常很大。Small sparks.
Big everyday.

花火工作室專注於 App 的構思、設計與開發。從一個需要解決的小問題，到一段值得留下的記憶，我們讓想法長成能陪伴日常的作品。FireSparkle.Studio dreams up, designs and builds apps. From a small problem worth solving to a memory worth keeping, we grow ideas into things that keep you company every day.

  1. 01

    從日常出發Start from everyday life

    留意生活裡的小需求，讓每個點子都有值得被做出來的理由。We notice the small needs in daily life, so every idea has a real reason to exist.

  2. 02

    簡約而精緻Simple, and finely made

    簡約，讓重要的事更清楚；精緻，讓每次打開都有一點愉悅。Simplicity keeps what matters clear; craft makes every launch a small delight.

  3. 03

    保持好奇Stay curious

    願意為平凡的生活多想一步，持續探索作品能帶來的不同可能。We think one step further for ordinary days and keep exploring what our apps could become.
